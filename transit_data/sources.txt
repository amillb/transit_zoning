subway_entrances_[YEAR].zip
A zipped shapefile of subway entrance locations for each year, for underground stations that do not have a defined parcel as part of the station. Stations that do have a defined parcel as part of the station (often, an on-ground entrance plaza), are not included in these shapefiles and are instead included in the parcel shapefiles (even if there are other entrances not within the defined parcel).
Created by authors from:
OpenStreetMap (2025). OpenStreetMap. Retrieved February 2, 2026, from https://www.openstreetmap.org/.
